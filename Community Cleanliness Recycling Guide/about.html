About Our Community Guide

A simple educational website that encourages responsible waste management and community cleanliness.

Why this website was created

Keeping a community clean requires awareness and cooperation. This website gathers basic information about waste segregation, recycling, and cleanliness activities in one accessible place.

It is designed as an educational guide and a front-end demonstration for the IT111 website development project.
