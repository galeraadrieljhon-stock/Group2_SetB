Frequently asked questions

What is waste segregation?

It is the practice of separating waste into categories to support proper handling and disposal.

Why should we recycle?

Recycling can help conserve materials and reduce the amount of waste sent for disposal.

What is the purpose of this website?

The purpose of this website is to educate the community about environmental responsibility and provide practical guidance on maintaining a clean and sustainable environment.
